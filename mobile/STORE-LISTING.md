## Description
Your whole company. One easy app.

Steelcap Pro runs small oilfield, pipeline and construction crews from the phone. Your guys do their safety forms and field tickets in the truck. You see everything from anywhere, and the billing goes straight into QuickBooks.

START MY DAY
One morning check-in (truck, site, crew, hazards) fills the pre-trip, FLHA and toolbox meeting. Check each one and send.

SAFETY FORMS
Pre-trip inspections, FLHAs, toolbox meetings and your own custom checklists. Photos and defects go straight to the owner.

FIELD TICKETS
Hours, helper and standby time, site, AFE and job notes in 30 seconds. The owner approves them from the phone.

DISPATCH AND CREW CHAT
Send tomorrow's site, truck and start time to the crew. Chat with the whole crew or one guy.

BILLING AND QUICKBOOKS
Approved tickets become invoice drafts and payroll hours in QuickBooks. Fuel and receipts become expenses. Or export to Excel or Google Sheets.

WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
Forms and tickets save on the phone and send themselves when you're back in range.

Built by a guy who runs picker trucks in northern Alberta and BC.

A Steelcap Pro company account is required. Owners start a 7-day free trial at steelcappro.com.
