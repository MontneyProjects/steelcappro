What is an FLHA?

A Field Level Hazard Assessment is a short check a crew does on site, right before the work starts. You look at the job you're about to do, write down what could hurt someone, and agree on how you'll control it. Everyone on the crew signs on, and you review it again if the job, the crew or the conditions change during the day.

When do you do one?

  • At the start of every shift, before work begins
  • When you move to a new site or a new task
  • When conditions change: weather, new equipment, a new crew member, a client change
  • After a near miss or anything that didn't go to plan

What goes on an FLHA

  1. Job details: date, location or LSD, client, supervisor and the task.
  2. Hazards on site: overhead lines, H2S, suspended loads, pinch points, slips and trips, weather and the rest.
  3. Task steps and controls: break the job into steps, list the hazard for each, rate the risk, and write how you'll control it.
  4. PPE and checks: what everyone is wearing, and that monitors, fall protection and fire extinguishers are checked.
  5. Crew sign-on: every person on site signs before starting.
  6. End of job review: site cleaned up, crew accounted for, incidents reported.

How to fill it out (2 minutes)

  1. Fill in the job details at the top before you leave the truck.
  2. Walk the site and tick every hazard you see.
  3. Break the job into 3 to 5 steps and write a control for each hazard.
  4. Check PPE and gear, then have every crew member sign on.
  5. At the end of the day, finish the review and hand it in.

Common questions

Is this FLHA template free?

Yes. Download it, print as many as you want and use it on your sites. No sign-up needed.

Does this FLHA meet OH&S requirements?

It covers the parts most FLHAs include, but every company, client and province or state has its own rules. Check it against your safety program and your client's requirements, and add anything they ask for.

What's the difference between an FLHA and a JSA?

A JSA (job safety analysis) is usually written ahead of time for a type of job. An FLHA is done on site by the crew, right before the work, for the conditions that day.

Who fills out the FLHA?

The crew doing the work, usually led by the foreman or lead hand. Everyone on site signs on.
